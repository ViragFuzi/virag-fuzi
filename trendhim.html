The brief

Make organic short-form videos for YouTube Shorts and TikTok that feel like Trendhim and give viewers something useful, not just an ad.

What I did

  • 01

    Came up with video ideas that fit the Trendhim brand.

  • 02

    Wrote the scripts and found the right hook for the opening seconds.

  • 03

    Shot and edited the videos in Premiere Pro, with fast-paced voiceovers.

  • 04

    Wrote the captions and copy.

  • 05

    Selected content for the Grin influencer program.

[Add: a still from one of the videos]

What I learned

  • The hook decides everything.

    If the opening doesn't grab people, nobody sees the rest.

  • Speed keeps people watching.

    A fast voiceover and tight edit leave no room to scroll away.

  • Value beats selling.

    My two biggest YouTube Shorts both teach something: how to tie a tie in under 10 seconds, and three ways to style a chain necklace.

  • Every video still has to feel like the brand.

    Being trendy isn't enough if it doesn't look like Trendhim.
